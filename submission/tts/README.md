## How to use

Open the clips on your computer. Answer the real CALL-E call on your authorized phone. Play hello, then the bakery offer when asked; use confirm after the read-back. On call two, use hello, collector accepts, then confirm. Place the computer speaker near the phone microphone, keep background sound low, and avoid playing over the caller.

Select **Sinhala** in the live call form. The application and enum/time fields remain English/24-hour format. This must be disclosed in the video as a TTS-assisted role-play. The phone route still has to be accepted by CALL-E; these clips do not bypass a destination restriction.

Do not trigger a call until the person answering is ready and the audio is audible. No calls are created by opening or playing these files.
