## Recording notes

- Real CALL-E conversations must finish successfully before filming the claimed real-call path.
- Role-playing humans or TTS respondents are acceptable integration-test counterparts only when clearly labeled. They do not establish real donor adoption or actual food rescue.
- Keep `.env`, private request bodies, account balances, tokens and unmasked numbers off screen.
- Public video must be on YouTube or Vimeo. Record in English, or add accurate English subtitles and translate all submission materials.
- Do not say “48 loaves rescued” unless a genuine collection took place. Use “48-loaf pickup agreed” for a real operation, or “fictional 48-loaf pickup agreed in the role-play” for this test.
